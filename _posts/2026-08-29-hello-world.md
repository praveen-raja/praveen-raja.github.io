Welcome to my corner of the web. I'm Praveenkumar Raja, a software engineer who
spends most of the day in **Java** and **Spring Boot**, building backend systems,
microservices, and multi-cloud integrations.

## Why a blog?

Writing forces clarity. I want a place to:

- Share things I learn while building enterprise-scale backends.
- Break down patterns I reach for &mdash; REST API design, messaging with
  ActiveMQ and Redis, ETL pipelines, and reliability practices like health checks
  and root cause analysis.
- Keep notes on cloud, containers, and the occasional security CTF.

## What to expect

Short, practical posts grounded in real work. No fluff, just the stuff I wish I'd
found sooner.

## Adding a new post (for me, later)

Drop a Markdown file in `_posts/` named `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`, add the front
matter block up top, write, and commit. GitHub Pages builds the rest.

```markdown
---
title: "My Next Post"
date: 2026-09-01 09:00:00 +0530
categories:
  - engineering
tags:
  - spring-boot
excerpt: "A one-line summary."
---

Post content goes here.
```
